My vision

Five Pillars for Navnirman of PFC

A sustainable model that future Executive Committees can build upon with confidence, rooted in democratic principles and operational excellence.

The Legislature

The General Body

In a democracy, the legislature writes the laws and controls the budget. In PFC, the legislature is 2520 members of association. Lets work on:

  • PFCAOA Election policy
  • PFCAOA Governance 2.0 policy
  • PFCAOA Vendor Management 2.0 Policy
  • PFCAOA No Confidence Policy

The Executive

The Board & Management

Managing daily operations efficiently and fairly, executing the will of the people without bias. I want to:

  • Audit: re-audit and resubmission of budget to general body.
  • Professional:standardize and publish EC report card at each AGM.
  • SLA to Respond:define EC response times for all resident requests via official channels.
  • Bridge the Gap: Leverage tech, process, sub-committees and data to execute tasks efficiently, fairly and transparently.
  • Accountability: implement a way as such each SGBM agenda will be backed by atleast one EC member which will be printed on pre-read .

The Judiciary

Audits & Compliance

Ensuring accountability, resolving disputes fairly, and providing a check on those in power.

  • Judiciary: EC must immediately form Adhoc independent Judicial Committee triggered by atleast 10% member signatures or audit miscompliance.
  • Actionable: EC Disciplinary & Performance policies.
  • Grievances: create a robust grievance Redressal system.

The Fourth Estate

Radical Transparency

The public's right to access truthful information. A government’s obligation to operate in the light.

  • Right to Inspect and Right to Information: Association office is just a custodian of information. Leverage tech to provide seamless yet secured access to financial records/vendor contracts/AMC agreements etc.
  • Meaningful Openhouse: Bi-monthly openhouse connects where residents get access to EC members and discuss issues.

Society

Resident Committees

Create Resident Committees to engage residents in community initiatives to make the community vibrant and leadership grounded.

  • Empowerment: Setup recognized committees/groups for security, water, health gardens, ammenities, policy, events, tenants and so on. This leads to quicker feedback and faster execution.
  • Engagement: Every member has a voice. Open communication and surveys for feedback and inputs.
