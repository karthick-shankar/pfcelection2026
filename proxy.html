Important Rules for Proxies

As per Byelaws (Ch. III, Sec. 1.c)

  • A Member entitled to vote can do so in person or through a proxy.
  • A proxy must be a resident of the development.
  • The proxy form (Annexure-1) must be submitted as a signed hard copy (signed by the person whose name appears first in the Sale Deed) by a resident owner, at least one hour before the start of the General Body Meeting/Election.
  • One resident may not carry more than two proxies for voting purposes.
  • A proxy who is not a Member shall not be entitled to speak at the meeting.

Important: Complete Your Proxy Form

Form is Incomplete Without Signature!

Note to Proxy Holders

As per the Byelaws Chapter III, Clause 1.c:

  • The proxy must be a current resident of the Prestige Falcon City Development.
  • This form must be submitted as follows:
    • A signed hard copy (signed by the person whose name appears first in the Sale Deed) must be submitted by a resident owner to the Estate Manager at the AOA office, at least one hour before the start of the General Body Meeting/Election.
      OR
    • A scanned copy of the signed proxy form must be emailed by the Member from their registered email address to: office@pfcaoa.com at least one hour before the start of the General Body Meeting / Elections.
  • Unsigned proxy forms or those sent from unregistered contact details will not be accepted.
  • Absolute Deadline: Proxy forms will NOT be accepted under any circumstances if submitted after 8th Oct 2026, 2:00 PM.
  • A resident may carry a maximum of two proxies.
  • A proxy who is not a Member is not entitled to speak at the meeting.
  • The Estate Manager will maintain a verified proxy register, and proxy ballots will only be issued to those listed in this authenticated proxy list.
  • Proxy forms signed by CAM defaulters / those with membership fee dues will not be considered.
